How Average Is Calculated

An average (also called arithmetic mean) is calculated by adding all numbers together and dividing by the count of numbers.

Average = (Sum of all numbers) ÷ (Count of numbers)

Average Calculator Example

Input:

10, 20, 30, 40, 50

Calculation:

(10 + 20 + 30 + 40 + 50) ÷ 5 = 150 ÷ 5 = 30

Result:

Average = 30

Types of Average

Arithmetic Mean

The sum of all numbers divided by the count. This is what our calculator computes. Most common type of average.

Median

The middle value when numbers are arranged in order. Useful when dealing with outliers.

Mode

The number that appears most frequently in a dataset. Useful for finding the most common value.

This calculator specifically computes the Arithmetic Mean.
