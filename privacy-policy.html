Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us

Last Updated: 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Average Calculator website. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Average Calculator tool.

2. Information We Collect

Input Data:

The numbers you enter into the calculator are processed entirely within your web browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit your input data to any server.

Browser Information:

We do not track your browsing behavior, device information, or location. The calculator works without collecting any identification information from you.

3. How We Use Information

Since we do not collect personal information or usage data, there is no information to use for any purpose beyond what is necessary for the calculator to function.

4. Data Processing Location

All calculations are performed locally on your device using JavaScript. Your numbers never leave your computer or device. We do not:

  • Send your numbers to any server
  • Store your calculations anywhere
  • Use any third-party services to process your data
  • Save any information about your calculations

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our Average Calculator does not use:

  • Cookies (persistent or temporary)
  • Analytics tracking
  • Advertising tracking
  • Session storage for your data

Each time you visit, the calculator starts fresh with no memory of previous visits or calculations.

6. Third-Party Services

The Average Calculator does not integrate with any third-party services such as:

  • Google Analytics
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media platforms
  • Cloud storage services
  • Any other external services

7. Security

Since all calculations happen locally in your browser, security is inherent to the system. Your numbers are never vulnerable to interception because they never travel over the internet.

We recommend:

  • Using a secure, updated web browser
  • Keeping your device software up to date
  • Not sharing your device if you enter sensitive information

8. Your Data Rights

Since we do not collect or store any personal information:

  • There is no data for us to share with you
  • There is no data for us to delete
  • You have complete control over your information (it stays on your device)

9. Children's Privacy

The Average Calculator is suitable for all ages, including children. Since we do not collect any personal information or identifiable data, there is no additional privacy consideration for children using our calculator.

Parents should supervise children's use of any online tools as they normally would.

10. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, you can:

  • Visit our About page
  • Visit our FAQ page for common questions
  • Review our Disclaimer for additional information

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will indicate when changes are made by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

Your continued use of the Average Calculator after any changes have been made constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

12. Important Notes

Your Responsibility:

You are responsible for keeping your device secure and protected. Since all calculations happen on your device, your privacy depends on:

  • Your device security settings
  • Your web browser security
  • Your device's physical security

Do Not Enter Unnecessary Personal Information:

While the calculator only processes numbers, we recommend:

  • Not using the calculator for sensitive personal data
  • Clearing your browser history if you're on a shared device
  • Using the calculator on a private device when possible

Third-Party Websites:

If you access our website through a third-party site or link, that site may have its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for third-party privacy practices. Please review the privacy policy of any referring site.

13. Compliance

This Average Calculator is designed with privacy as a core principle:

  • No data collection means no data breaches
  • No third-party integrations means no external data sharing
  • No tracking means no intrusive monitoring
  • Local processing means maximum privacy and security

14. Summary

The Average Calculator is designed with your privacy in mind:

  • ✓ All calculations happen locally on your device
  • ✓ No data is collected, stored, or transmitted
  • ✓ No cookies or tracking of any kind
  • ✓ No third-party services or integrations
  • ✓ Completely free and private to use

We believe privacy is a right, not a feature. Our tool is built on this principle.

Thank you for using the Average Calculator. Your privacy is important to us, and we're committed to maintaining the highest standards of data protection and privacy.
