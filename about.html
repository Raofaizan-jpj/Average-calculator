What is an Average?

An average, also known as the arithmetic mean, is a measure of the central value of a set of numbers. It represents the typical or middle value in your data. The average is one of the most common statistical measures used in mathematics, science, business, and everyday situations.

How it's calculated: Add all the numbers together, then divide by how many numbers you have.

Example:

Numbers: 10, 20, 30
Sum: 10 + 20 + 30 = 60
Count: 3
Average: 60 ÷ 3 = 20
